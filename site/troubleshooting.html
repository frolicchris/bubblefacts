Troubleshooting

Something not working? Find what you're seeing in the What you see column. Most problems have a quick fix.

Troubleshooting by symptom
What you seeLikely reasonWhat to do
Mac says "BubbleFacts" Not Opened, or that Apple can't check it The app isn't signed yet Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. On older Macs, right-click the app, choose Open, then Open again.
Windows says "Windows protected your PC" The app isn't signed yet Click More info, then Run anyway.
A small red dot in the bottom-right corner The overlay can't reach the app Open BubbleFacts. If it's already open, check the Stream light on the dashboard, or quit it from the menu bar or tray and open it again.
Nothing at all, not even a red dot OBS isn't loading the overlay First click Show a test bubble. If it appears, OBS is fine: that song just had no reliable facts, and the dashboard says so. If it doesn't, check the source is visible (the eye icon in Sources). Then drag the tile from BubbleFacts into OBS again. If you added it by hand, check it uses Local file and points at the file shown under Help and troubleshooting.
The dashboard says "Connecting to OBS", then "Add BubbleFacts to OBS" BubbleFacts just started and OBS hasn't picked it up yet, or OBS doesn't have it If BubbleFacts is already in OBS, wait a few seconds. If it still asks you to add it, check OBS is open and the BubbleFacts source is in the current scene, or drag the tile into OBS again.
No test bubble, and no "It's on your stream!" OBS didn't load the overlay, or BubbleFacts runs on a different computer BubbleFacts needs to run on the same computer as OBS. Try dragging the tile again, or add it by hand.
The app says StreamerSongList rejected your sign-in or token Your sign-in ran out, or the token is wrong or expired Sign in again from the app's Settings, or paste a new token if you use one (see Having trouble signing in?).
The app says StreamElements didn't accept your JWT token The token was reset or pasted wrong Get the token again (see Use StreamElements instead) and paste it in Settings, under Your song list. Then click Save.
"Reconnecting to StreamElements" while a song plays StreamElements says a song is playing, but BubbleFacts can't see which one Nothing, usually. If it lasts about two more minutes, BubbleFacts restarts its connection on its own. Your song queue isn't touched.
The app says "The AI download paused" Not enough disk space, the connection dropped, or the file didn't match the one BubbleFacts expects If the connection dropped, the app tries again on its own. If you're low on space, free up at least 4 GB. If the file didn't match, click Try again later; if it keeps happening, ask in Discussions. Or choose Online, with Groq (free key) for now (see Optional: use an online AI instead).
No facts about the song, or none at all No source knew the song BubbleFacts reads your whole song list once to learn how you write it: the game in the artist field, or "Game - Track" with the composer as artist, or "Track (Film)", and so on. If a song still gets the wrong match, the surest form is the game's name in the artist field. With StreamElements, clearly titled videos ("Artist - Song") work best. Obscure songs often have no article, so they get custom facts instead, or none until you add your own.
Few or no facts, soon after installing BubbleFacts is still getting ready Until it's ready, songs get facts from music databases and your own facts. The Facts light on the dashboard shows the progress.
Fewer bubbles than expected Some captions failed the fact check That's normal for songs with short articles. It means the check is working.
The banner is cut off, or the bubbles sit in the wrong place The source isn't the size of your canvas Remove the source and drag the tile from BubbleFacts into OBS again. Or right-click the source, choose Properties, and set Width and Height to your canvas size (see adding it by hand).
The source is blank, or stops updating An OBS Browser source setting, or an old copy of the page Right-click the source, choose Properties, and click Refresh cache of current page. Leave Shutdown source when not visible and Refresh browser when scene becomes active off, and don't clear the Custom CSS that OBS fills in.
Viewers on phones say the bubbles are small Phone screens are small In Settings, then Bubbles, set Bubble size to Large or Larger.
Your Mac asks for permission every time BubbleFacts opens Allow was clicked, which covers one time only Click Always Allow the next time it asks. It asks once after each update.
You need the bubbles off the screen right now Anything Click Pause bubbles on the dashboard, or in the menu bar or tray menu.
Your stream stutters when a song starts The built-in AI is sharing your computer with OBS Choose Online, with Groq (free key) in Settings, under Advanced, then Who writes the facts (see Optional: use an online AI instead). It's free and does the work online.
The app says the built-in AI can't run on this computer The processor or graphics can't run it, even in processor-only mode Click Switch to Groq in the app's message. It's free. See the system requirements.
A new version has a problem and you want the one before Something changed that you rely on Quit BubbleFacts, download the earlier version from the list of all versions, and install it over the new one. Your settings and facts work with it; a setting the earlier version doesn't have is just left out. Then please tell us what went wrong.
You're moving to a new computer Your settings and facts are on the old one On the old computer, open Settings, then Advanced, and click Back up…. Install BubbleFacts on the new one, sign in, then use Restore… in the same place and pick that file. Your sign-in and keys aren't in the backup, so you sign in again. If you used an online AI, choose it again in Settings.

Still stuck? In the app, open Help and troubleshooting and click Report a problem, or ask in the Q&A on GitHub Discussions. The help page lists every option.
