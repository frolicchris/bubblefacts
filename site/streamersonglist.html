Song sources

Song facts overlay for StreamerSongList

BubbleFacts reads the song you are playing from your StreamerSongList queue and shows short facts about it as bubbles on your stream, from Wikipedia and open music databases. You sign in once with the Twitch account you use on StreamerSongList; there is no token to copy.

BubbleFacts is free and open source for musicians and DJs who stream, on Mac, Windows and Linux. Every fact is screened against its source, and one click on Wrong takes it off your stream.

Set it up with StreamerSongList

  1. Install BubbleFacts from the download page and open it.
  2. Under Where do your song requests come from?, leave StreamerSongList picked.
  3. Click Sign in with StreamerSongList and sign in with the Twitch account you use on StreamerSongList. Back in BubbleFacts, your channel is filled in for you.
  4. Open OBS Studio and choose the scene you normally stream from.
  5. In BubbleFacts, drag the tile that says Drag this into your Sources list onto the Sources list in OBS. A test bubble shows up and the app says It's on your stream!
