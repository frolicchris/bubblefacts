Song sources

Move your song list on without the mouse

BubbleFacts follows your song list and never changes it. So when a song ends, your song list has to move on to the next song, and BubbleFacts follows. Hands on your instrument? Most song lists have a chat command, so a mod or a stream bot can do it for you.

The command for each song source

How to move on to the next song, for each song source
Song sourceChat commandWho can use itOr click
StreamerSongList !setPlayed (your list may also take !done). It marks the song that's playing now as played. You. Whether your mods can is set in StreamerSongList; its public pages don't say who can by default. Mark the song played on your queue at streamersonglist.com.
StreamElements Media Request !skip. It skips the song that's playing now. You and your mods, unless you changed it. The Media Request player on your StreamElements dashboard.
Nightbot song requests !songs skip. It skips the song that's playing now. You and your mods, unless you changed it. The player on Nightbot's Song Requests page, or the Fast Forward media key while Nightbot's desktop app runs.
Streamling None. Streamling shows what your music player is playing. Whoever controls your music player. Skip to the next song in your music player.
What's Now Playing, or another app's file None. The file changes when the app that writes it moves on. Whoever controls that app. Play the next track in your DJ software or music app.

Let a mod move it on

  1. Find your song source in the table above.
  2. Check that the command is turned on and that your mods may use it:
    • StreamerSongList: sign in at streamersonglist.com and look through your commands for Marks the current now-playing song as played. It lists the names it answers to. Viewers see the same list at streamersonglist.com/t/your channel/commands.
    • StreamElements: on your StreamElements dashboard, go to Chatbot, then Chat Commands, then Default Commands, and find !skip.
    • Nightbot: in Nightbot's control panel, open the default commands page and find !songs. Its options button sets who may use it.
  3. Tell your mods the command, and when to use it: once you've clearly moved on to the next song.
  4. Try it once before you go live. Start a song, have a mod send the command in chat, and watch On stream now on the BubbleFacts dashboard change to the next song.

Let a stream bot do it

A stream bot, or a button set up to post in your chat, can send the same command. Your song list treats it like anyone else's message:

  • If it posts as your own account, the command counts as yours.
  • If it posts as its own account, that account needs a mod's rights. Make it a mod in your channel.

Streamling and now playing files have no chat command. There, a bot can only help if it can control your music player or DJ software.

If nobody moves it on

BubbleFacts notices. When your song list shows the same song for a long time (10 minutes, unless you change it), BubbleFacts stops that song's bubbles and tells any app you share facts with that the song may have changed. Both start again by themselves with your next song. Play long medleys? Open Settings, then Bubbles, and raise Take a song as over after (minutes). It goes from 5 to 60.

A now playing file that hasn't changed in 15 minutes counts as no song playing.

Where these commands come from

Song lists change their commands from time to time. If one here doesn't work, check that song list's own help:

  • StreamerSongList: the commands list for your channel at streamersonglist.com
  • StreamElements: the !skip command and the song request commands
  • Nightbot: the !songs command, default commands and the Nightbot app
