Song sources

Song facts overlay from a now playing text file

Does your music app write the song that is playing to a text file? BubbleFacts follows that file, with no sign-in and no token, and shows short facts about each song as bubbles on your stream. DJs: most DJ software needs a helper app, such as What's Now Playing, to write that file. Brand-new club tracks may get fewer facts.

BubbleFacts is free and open source for musicians and DJs who stream, on Mac, Windows and Linux. Every fact is screened against its source, and one click on Wrong takes it off your stream.

Set it up with a now playing file

  1. Install BubbleFacts from the download page and open it. Under Where do your song requests come from?, choose Now playing file.
  2. Pick Another app's file, click Choose file, and select the text file your app writes the song to. (Using What's Now Playing? Pick What's Now Playing instead, and there is nothing to choose.)
  3. Tell BubbleFacts how the line reads: Artist - Title, or Title only. Then click Continue.
  4. Open OBS Studio and choose the scene you normally stream from.
  5. In BubbleFacts, drag the tile that says Drag this into your Sources list onto the Sources list in OBS. A test bubble shows up and the app says It's on your stream!

DJ software

  • VirtualDJ: in What's Now Playing settings, choose Virtual DJ as the source, then check its History and Playlists folders. Then pick What's Now Playing in BubbleFacts.
  • Mixxx: What's Now Playing reads Mixxx's Live Broadcasting stream (use Ogg Vorbis or Opus so the song names come through). Then pick What's Now Playing in BubbleFacts.
  • Serato DJ, Traktor, djay Pro and DJUCED: choose your app as the source in What's Now Playing settings, then pick What's Now Playing in BubbleFacts. (Traktor also needs its broadcasting turned on, as the What's Now Playing docs explain.)
  • Denon DJ hardware (Prime and SC series): What's Now Playing connects to it over your network.
  • The Now Playing app (tested with Mixxx by a beta tester DJ): add a File Output with the template {{artist}} - {{title}}. Then pick Another app's file in BubbleFacts and choose that file. File Output needs the app's Pro version for now.

Setup steps for each DJ app are in the What's Now Playing docs.
