Changelog

What's new

What changed in each version. The full notes for every release are on GitHub.

Version 2.0.0-beta.11 Beta, October 3, 2026

Wrong after the song, facts from another music content creator's channel, and a safety check of everything.

  • New: Earlier songs on the dashboard. Spotted a wrong fact while playing? Mark it Wrong after the song, no need to stop.
  • New: Connect Twitch (optional, in Settings). Then, for another music content creator's song, Fill from their Twitch About fills in facts from their channel's About, for you to check before saving.
  • Safer: after a security review of the whole app, a bubble can never show a link, a chat command or an @mention, whatever a request's title says. Only OBS can connect to BubbleFacts. The built-in AI only accepts the exact file it expects. Restoring a backup never changes which AI you use.
  • Fixed: Show a test bubble during a song no longer clears that song's bubbles.
  • Fixed: a fact marked Wrong stays off even if BubbleFacts restarts, and Pause bubbles stays on if BubbleFacts has to recover from a problem mid-song.
  • Improved: OBS picks BubbleFacts back up within a few seconds when you open it, and the app says "Connecting to OBS" meanwhile instead of asking you to add it.
  • Improved: Add facts for another song is a button on the dashboard, and About your own compositions opens right under I play my own compositions.
  • Improved: clearer setup wording, a Groq key hint, and Troubleshooting and Ask on Discord buttons in the app's help.

After updating, right-click the BubbleFacts source in OBS and choose Refresh.

Version 2.0.0-beta.10 Beta, October 2, 2026

  • Fixed: on a Mac, after an update, the request for your password could still appear before the BubbleFacts window (beta.7's fix missed one early check). Now the window opens first and tells you to click Always Allow.

Version 2.0.0-beta.9 Beta, October 2, 2026

Your facts are backed up for you, and bubble timing says what it does.

  • New: BubbleFacts backs up your settings and facts by itself whenever they change, and keeps the last 20. Back up… and Restore… move them to another computer. Your sign-in is never in a backup.
  • Improved: the bubble timing settings now read A new bubble every (seconds) and Each bubble stays up (seconds), with a line saying whether bubbles show one at a time or overlap.
  • Improved: the About page says BubbleFacts isn't affiliated with Twitch.

Thanks to izzyparadox for the timing question that led to the clearer labels.

Version 2.0.0-beta.8 Beta, October 2, 2026

Bubbles that really sit at the bottom, and an About page.

  • Fixed: with Where bubbles appear set to the bottom, bubbles showed just below the middle of the screen. They now sit on the bottom edge, and the first ones of a song keep clear of the Now Playing bubble.
  • New: an About page, also in the tray menu, with credits, thanks, and the license of everything BubbleFacts is built on and where its facts come from.

Thanks to izzyparadox for spotting where the bubbles really landed.

Version 2.0.0-beta.7 Beta, October 2, 2026

A sign-in fix, two settings testers asked for, and no more repeated bubbles. (There was no public beta.6: its fixes are all here.)

  • Fixed: signing in with StreamerSongList could stop at step 2 with "Connected to ." and a blank overlay. BubbleFacts now starts as soon as you're signed in.
  • Fixed: a live learn typed as a video title got no facts with the built-in AI.
  • Fixed: a full disk while downloading an update now shows a message.
  • Changed: notes about your own compositions show only when the song's artist is your channel name or Your name on stream. A friend's piece tagged "Originals" no longer gets them.
  • New: choose where bubbles appear: anywhere, or the top, bottom, left or right of the screen. Settings, then Bubbles.
  • Mac: after an update, the window opens before your Mac asks to use your saved sign-in, and tells you to click Always Allow.
  • A restart, or reloading the BubbleFacts source in OBS, no longer replays bubbles the song already showed.
  • New: turn off the Now Playing bubble if your own overlay already shows the song. Settings, then Bubbles.
  • More bubbles per song: true facts that only mention a game or a colleague are no longer held back, and an award is checked against who won it.
  • Game tracks draw on the game's music article when it has one, so two songs from one game get different facts.
  • A fellow streamer's piece (an @handle as the artist) is no longer looked up.
  • The log lists each fact shown and its source.

Thanks to izzyparadox for finding the sign-in bug and asking for the two new settings.

Version 2.0.0-beta.5 Beta, October 1, 2026

Facts with more color, sources you can check, and an app that updates itself.

  • More interesting facts: what the people who made the music said about it, who and what influenced it, how it's built (with music terms explained in plain words), and how it did on the charts and at awards.
  • See where a fact came from: click its source on the dashboard to read the sentence it was written from and open the article.
  • Updates from inside the app: Update now downloads and checks the new version, and Install now swaps it in. Nothing installs without your click. This starts with the version after this one.
  • Live learns get facts when BubbleFacts can find the song, under the LIVE LEARN banner.
  • Your originals lead with your own notes about your compositions. "Hasn't come up in N days" is gone.
  • BubbleFacts learns how your song list is written: the game in the artist field, "Game - Track" with the composer, "Track (Film)", and more.
  • Game tracks with their own Wikipedia article use it, and a song's article is preferred to its album's.
  • Add facts for this song has one box per fact, so a long fact stays one fact. Your own facts get an Edit button where they show.
  • Saving a setting no longer restarts BubbleFacts mid-song unless the change needs it.

Version 2.0.0-beta.4 Beta, October 1, 2026

Facts that tell viewers something new, a shorter setup, and custom facts for one song.

  • No more facts viewers already know: a caption that only repeats the title and the artist on the request is skipped.
  • Setup ends as soon as the test bubble shows: click Start using BubbleFacts. Originals and live learns are optional, and in Settings.
  • Custom facts for one song, artist or game: start the line with its name in brackets, like [Song of Storms] It plays in a windmill. It shows only for that, first.
  • Add facts for this song works before a show: click Preparing a different song? and type its title and artist. Settings lists every song you've done this for, with Edit and Remove. A creator's link always gets its bubble.
  • The dashboard says why a song is quiet: still looking, no reliable facts, or a live learn. Quiet is normal.
  • Stricter fact check: a credit needs the article to tie that person to that role, not just mention both.
  • Shorter Settings: the example fact packs and the choice of AI are under Advanced. The instrument setting is gone.
  • An online AI, if you choose one, is sent the song and the article only. Nothing about you.
  • Send a beta test report under Help and troubleshooting fills in your version and computer for you.

Version 2.0.0-beta.3 Beta, September 30, 2026

StreamElements song requests, a Wrong button, and more facts for songs Wikipedia doesn't know.

  • StreamElements works as a song source, besides StreamerSongList. Choose it in setup or in Settings and paste your JWT token. BubbleFacts reads the artist and song from each request's YouTube title, and reads YouTube's auto-generated uploads exactly.
  • A Wrong button next to each fact under On stream now. One click takes the fact off your stream, and BubbleFacts never uses that fact's source for that song again, even after a restart. Undo if you clicked by mistake, and Report it (opens GitHub) if you'd like to tell us. It replaces Report this fact.
  • Add facts for this song: give any song its own facts, like another streamer's original. They show exactly as you write them, and a songwriter is only credited when you name one.
  • The dashboard says where each fact came from: Wikipedia, Wikidata, MusicBrainz, your song list or your own facts.
  • Songs with no Wikipedia article can get facts from Wikidata, then MusicBrainz. These are plain sentences filled in from their data, with no AI involved. Titles in Japanese, Korean, Chinese or Cyrillic are looked up as written.
  • Backup facts are now called custom facts. The example packs are off by default, and with no custom facts, a song no source knows gets no bubbles.
  • Better matching: fewer wrong articles, such as a band matched to a game with a similar name. When only the artist's article exists, facts about the song itself come first.
  • Stricter fact check: a caption saying who composed, wrote or directed something needs the article to say so.
  • Better facts: no critics' opinions, nothing about the music video, no repeats from earlier songs, and no song length.
  • Copy credit line under Help and troubleshooting, for your stream panel or video descriptions.
  • Pausing a StreamElements song no longer stops BubbleFacts. If a song is playing but not followed, the dashboard says so, and BubbleFacts reconnects on its own.
  • Linux: the built-in AI no longer crashes, and the app warns you if your computer has no keyring to protect your saved token.

Thanks to SaxDragon for testing StreamElements.

Version 2.0.0 Coming soon

The desktop app, and a new name: Stream Facts is now BubbleFacts. Download it, open it, and click through setup. No Terminal needed.

  • Installers for Mac, Windows and Linux
  • A built-in AI that downloads on its own, with nothing extra to install
  • A two-step setup: connect your songs, then put BubbleFacts on your stream by dragging one tile into OBS
  • Works right away: songs get facts from Wikidata, MusicBrainz and your own facts while the built-in AI downloads
  • Add your own facts in Settings, or for one song
  • A dashboard showing what's playing and the facts shown, with Pause bubbles and Show a test bubble
  • Wrong, Report it and Report a problem buttons
  • Fixes itself: if something stops, it restarts on its own
  • Runs in the menu bar or system tray
  • Tells you when a new version is out
