What's new

What changed in each version. The full notes for every release are on GitHub.

Version 2.0.0-beta.3 Coming soon

StreamElements song requests, a Wrong button, and more facts for songs Wikipedia doesn't know.

  • StreamElements works as a song source, besides StreamerSongList. Choose it in setup or in Settings and paste your JWT token. BubbleFacts reads the artist and song from each request's YouTube title.
  • With a YouTube key built in, BubbleFacts reads the exact artist and song of YouTube's auto-generated uploads
  • A Wrong button next to each fact under On stream now. One click takes the fact off your stream, and BubbleFacts never uses that Wikipedia article for that song again, even after a restart. Tell us about it opens a report if you'd like.
  • Songs with no Wikipedia article can get facts from Wikidata, then MusicBrainz. These are plain sentences filled in from their data, with no AI involved.
  • When only the artist's article exists, facts about the song itself come first
  • The example backup facts are off by default. With no backup facts, a song no source knows gets none.
  • Better facts: no critics' opinions, nothing about the music video, no repeats from earlier songs, and no more song length
  • Pausing a StreamElements song no longer stops BubbleFacts. If a song is playing but not followed, the dashboard says so, and BubbleFacts reconnects on its own.
  • Linux: the built-in AI no longer crashes

Thanks to SaxDragon for testing StreamElements.

Release notes on GitHub

Version 2.0.0 Coming soon

The desktop app, and a new name: Stream Facts is now BubbleFacts. Download it, open it, and click through setup. No Terminal needed.

  • Installers for Mac, Windows and Linux
  • A built-in AI that downloads on its own, with nothing extra to install
  • A three-step setup: connect your songs, put BubbleFacts on your stream by dragging one tile into OBS, and, if you like, tell it about your music
  • Works right away: songs get backup facts while the built-in AI downloads
  • Add your own backup facts in Settings
  • A dashboard showing what's playing and the facts shown, with Pause bubbles and Show a test bubble
  • Report this fact and Report a problem buttons
  • Fixes itself: if something stops, it restarts on its own
  • Runs in the menu bar or system tray
  • Tells you when a new version is out
