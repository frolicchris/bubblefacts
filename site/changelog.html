What changed in each version. The full notes for every release are on GitHub.

Version 2.0.0 Coming soon

The desktop app, and a new name: Stream Facts is now BubbleFacts. Download it, open it, and click through setup. No Terminal needed.

  • Installers for Mac, Windows and Linux
  • A built-in AI that downloads on its own, with nothing extra to install
  • A three-step setup: sign in with StreamerSongList, tell it about your music, and add it to OBS
  • Add your own backup facts in Settings
  • A dashboard showing what's playing and the facts shown
  • That fact is wrong and Report a problem buttons
  • Fixes itself: if something stops, it restarts on its own
  • Runs in the menu bar or system tray
  • Tells you when a new version is out
