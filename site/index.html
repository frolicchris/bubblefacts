  1. Connect your songs

    Sign in with StreamerSongList, using your Twitch account.

  2. Add BubbleFacts to OBS

    Drag it from the app into your OBS Sources list.

You'll know it worked right away: a test bubble appears in OBS as soon as you add it.

No coding. No Terminal. No AI setup required. BubbleFacts gets its built-in fact writer ready in the background.

Want to customize it? Tell BubbleFacts about your originals, live learns, and your own facts for songs Wikipedia doesn't know. Or read the step-by-step guide.

How BubbleFacts checks its facts

AI can make things up. BubbleFacts is built to keep made-up facts off your stream, so its fact writer never answers from memory.

  • It finds the source first. When a song starts, it looks up the song, or the game or film it's from, on Wikipedia.
  • It writes only from that article. The fact writer rephrases details from the article and adds nothing of its own.
  • It drops anything the article doesn't support before it reaches your stream.
  • No article, no AI. If BubbleFacts can't find reliable facts for a song, it shows your own messages instead.

How this was made

BubbleFacts was written by Claude Code, Anthropic's AI coding agent, from a musician's direction. Almost none of the code was typed by hand. A person decided what it should do, tested it on live streams, and reported what went wrong.

The captions are written by a small AI model (Meta's Llama 3.2) from Wikipedia text.

It's shared as a useful tool, not as a claim of hand-written craft. Feedback and fact corrections from other musicians are welcome. BubbleFacts is open source on GitHub.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. BubbleFacts is free and open source, and its built-in fact writer costs nothing to run.

Is it safe and private?

Yes. You sign in to StreamerSongList in your own web browser, so BubbleFacts never sees your Twitch password. Your sign-in is stored encrypted on your computer. BubbleFacts looks song titles up on Wikipedia, and nothing is sent to this project. The privacy page has the details.

Do I need to know coding?

No. You download an app, sign in, and drag it into OBS. If you can add a webcam to OBS, you can do this. The setup guide walks you through every click.

What if a song isn't on Wikipedia?

BubbleFacts shows your own messages instead: short facts you write yourself (the app calls them backup facts). It comes with a few example facts, but they're only examples. They're short, they aren't updated or maintained, and they won't know your songs. So add your own. They go on stream exactly as you write them, so only add facts you've checked. See how in the setup guide.

Can BubbleFacts run on a different computer from OBS?

Not yet. BubbleFacts needs to run on the same computer as OBS. Two-PC setups, such as a music PC plus a streaming PC, aren't supported yet.

Does it work with Streamlabs Desktop?

It should, but it isn't tested yet. Dragging the tile may not work there. Instead, add a Browser source by hand: follow the add-it-by-hand steps. Only OBS Studio is tested.
