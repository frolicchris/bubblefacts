  • Wikidata

    For songs without a Wikipedia article, some facts come from Wikidata, whose structured data is in the public domain (CC0).

  • MusicBrainz

    When neither has the song, BubbleFacts uses release dates, albums and composers from MusicBrainz, whose core data is in the public domain (CC0).

  • Wikipedia

    Facts are rewritten from Wikipedia articles, whose text is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0. Bare facts, like a year or a composer's name, aren't covered by copyright, but wording taken from an article can be, and then the license asks you to credit Wikipedia. Captions are short rewrites, so most never reach that line, but crediting the sources keeps you safe.
