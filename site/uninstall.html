Uninstall

Uninstall

Removing BubbleFacts takes three parts: take it out of OBS, remove the app, and, if you want, delete its settings and AI model.

1

Remove it from OBS

  1. In OBS, find the BubbleFacts Browser source under Sources.
  2. Right-click it and choose Remove.
  3. Do this in every scene where you added it.
2

Remove the app

If you turned on start at login, turn it off in the app's settings first.

Mac

  1. Click the BubbleFacts icon in the menu bar and choose Quit.
  2. Open your Applications folder and drag BubbleFacts to the Trash.

Windows

  1. Click the BubbleFacts icon in the system tray and choose Quit.
  2. Open Settings, then Apps.
  3. Find BubbleFacts and choose Uninstall.

Linux

  1. Quit BubbleFacts from its icon in the system tray.
  2. If you used the AppImage, delete the BubbleFacts .AppImage file.
  3. If you installed the .deb, run this in a terminal:
    sudo apt remove bubblefacts
3

Delete settings and the AI model (optional)

Removing the app leaves your settings, your own backup facts, your encrypted sign-in, token and keys, the 2 GB AI model and the logs in a folder on your computer. That way, reinstalling picks up where you left off. To free the space and remove everything, delete this folder:

Where BubbleFacts keeps its settings and AI model
SystemFolderHow to get there
Mac ~/Library/Application Support/BubbleFacts In Finder, choose Go, then Go to Folder (Cmd+Shift+G), and paste the folder.
Windows %APPDATA%\BubbleFacts Paste the folder into the address bar of File Explorer and press Enter.
Linux ~/.config/BubbleFacts In your file manager, press Ctrl+H to show hidden folders, then open .config.

Not coming back?

If you used a token, deleting the folder removes the copy on your computer, but the token still works. To switch it off, sign in at streamersonglist.com, go to Settings, then Access, and delete it. Do the same for any Groq or Anthropic key on their websites.
