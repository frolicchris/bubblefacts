Requirements

System requirements

Most computers that comfortably run OBS should work. BubbleFacts checks by itself whether its built-in AI can run on your computer, and switches to a lighter mode if not. There's also a free online option. Here are the details.

Same computer as OBS

BubbleFacts needs to run on the same computer as OBS. Two-PC setups, such as a music PC plus a streaming PC, aren't supported yet.

High quality AI (optional)

In Settings, under Advanced, the built-in AI can be Standard or High quality. Standard is the default and suits most computers. High quality makes about half as many mistakes, but it's a bigger download, needs more memory and a strong graphics chip, and writes more slowly. You can switch at any time: BubbleFacts downloads the other one while the one you have keeps writing facts, then deletes the old one to save space. Switching back downloads it again.

Standard and High quality built-in AI compared
WhatStandard (default)High quality
AI model Meta's Llama 3.2 3B Meta's Llama 3.1 8B
Memory (RAM) 8 GB 16 GB recommended. It uses about 6 GB while it writes facts.
Disk space for the AI About 2.5 GB About 5 GB
Graphics Not required A graphics chip that can hold the AI, to be fast enough: any Apple silicon Mac (M1 or newer), or on Windows and Linux a graphics card with about 6 GB or more of its own memory (VRAM). Without one, it runs on the processor and can be several times slower.
Speed A few seconds per song on a recent computer About 1.5 to 2 times slower than Standard. BubbleFacts stops writing a song's facts after 90 seconds, so on a computer without a suitable graphics chip you may get fewer bubbles per song.

If your computer has less than 16 GB of memory, or is a Mac with an Intel processor, Settings warns you. You can still choose High quality, but it may run slowly or not load. If it can't run, BubbleFacts tells you and offers to switch back to Standard.

How fast are the facts?

When a song starts, the built-in AI takes a few seconds to write its facts. On the one computer measured so far, that's 5 to 8 seconds, well within the song. Other computers will vary until they're measured. These times are for Standard; High quality takes about 1.5 to 2 times as long.

If the built-in AI can't run

Some computers, for example ones with an older processor, can't run the built-in AI. You can still use BubbleFacts:

  1. On Windows and Linux, the app tries a simpler mode that uses only the processor. This happens on its own.
  2. If that doesn't work either, the app tells you, and its Switch to Groq button opens Settings. Groq is free, with a daily limit, and does the AI work online.

Groq is also a good choice if your computer already works hard while you stream. You can switch any time in Settings, under Advanced, then Who writes the facts. See using an online AI.

Advanced

If you already run Ollama, you can point BubbleFacts at it under Settings, then Advanced, then Who writes the facts. Install Ollama from ollama.com with its normal installer. BubbleFacts downloads the Ollama model for you the first time.

Processor details

For reference, these are the oldest processors BubbleFacts is expected to handle:

  • Mac: any Apple silicon Mac, or an Intel Mac from 2017 or newer
  • Windows and Linux: a 64-bit processor from 2015 or newer (Intel 6th generation Core or newer, or AMD Ryzen)
