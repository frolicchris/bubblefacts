Connect your songs

BubbleFacts watches your song requests, so it knows what you're playing.

Where do your song requests come from?

In StreamElements, open your dashboard, then Account, then Channels, and click Show secrets. Copy the JWT token. It works like a password: keep it off your stream.

You'll sign in with Twitch in your browser, then come back here.

Having trouble signing in?

You can connect with a Streamer Access Token instead. Find it at streamersonglist.com under Settings, then Access. It works like a password, so keep it off your stream.

Put BubbleFacts on your stream

Open OBS and choose the scene you normally stream from.

    You and your music
    Custom facts: facts that may appear during any song

    When no source knows a song, BubbleFacts shows a few of these, whatever the song is. So write facts that fit any song. With none, that song gets no bubbles, which is best for request channels. They go on stream exactly as you write them, so only add facts you've checked. For a fact about one song, artist or game, start the line with its name in brackets: it then shows only for that, first. Or use Add facts for this song on the dashboard.
