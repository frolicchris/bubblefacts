Connect your songs

BubbleFacts watches your StreamerSongList queue, so it knows what you're playing.

You'll sign in with Twitch in your browser, then come back here.

Having trouble signing in?

You can connect with a Streamer Access Token instead. Find it at streamersonglist.com under Settings, then Access. It works like a password, so keep it off your stream.

Put BubbleFacts on your stream

Open OBS and choose the scene you normally stream from.

Drag this into your Sources list
OBS adds it at the right size for your canvas.
… That's it. BubbleFacts will check that it's working.
Add it by hand
  1. Under Sources, click + and choose Browser. Name it "BubbleFacts".
  2. Check Local file, click Browse, and choose this file:
  3. Set Width and Height to your canvas size, usually 1920 and 1080. Click OK.

Dropping the tile does nothing on Windows? OBS may be running as administrator. Open OBS the usual way, or use these steps.
