Connect your song list

BubbleFacts follows your StreamerSongList queue, so it knows which song you're playing.

Opens your browser. Sign in with the Twitch account you use on StreamerSongList, then come back here.

Or connect with a token

Find it at streamersonglist.com under Settings, then Access. It works like a password: keep it off your stream.

Your music

Two quick questions, then your backup facts.

Backup facts

When a song has no Wikipedia article, BubbleFacts shows backup facts instead.

BubbleFacts comes with only a few example facts. They're short, they aren't updated, and they won't know your songs. Adding facts about the music you play makes your stream better.

Add it to OBS

One last thing, in OBS Studio. It takes about a minute.

  1. In the scene you stream from, under Sources, click + and choose Browser. Name it "BubbleFacts".
  2. Check Local file, click Browse, and choose this file:
  3. Set Width to 1920 and Height to 1080. Click OK.
  4. Right-click the new source and choose Transform, then Reset Transform.
